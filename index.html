06 / WORKFORCE ANALYTICS
WORKFORCE TURNOVER ANALYSIS

Analyzed turnover patterns across 372 employees to identify retention risk drivers. 29% overall turnover was not evenly distributed — Finance led at 39.8%, nearly double Operations' 19.7%. 75% of departures were voluntary, pointing to compensation and career growth as primary levers. Identified 8 high-risk managers with turnover rates ≥35%, with a moderate correlation (r ≈ 0.39) between promotion stagnation and attrition.
