01 / FINANCIAL PLANNING · 3-STAGE PIPELINE
INVESTMENT RISK & RETIREMENT PLANNING

A connected 3-stage Python pipeline that mirrors a real financial advisory workflow. Stage 1: a 6-factor questionnaire scores client risk tolerance and outputs a Conservative / Moderate / Aggressive classification. Stage 2: the suggested allocation is stress-tested against 5 years of live market data via the yfinance API — calculating Sharpe ratio, volatility, and correlation. Stage 3: a Monte Carlo engine runs 10,000 simulations projecting 30-year retirement outcomes, capturing sequence-of-returns risk that simple average-return models miss entirely.

"Risk score drives allocation, allocation maps to a stress-tested retirement scenario, and real correlation data shows what that allocation actually diversifies against."

ABOUT ME

I'm Nikolas J. Zeiner, a Business Analytics graduate based in Chandler, AZ with a Master of Science in Business Analytics (GPA 3.31) and a BS in Business Analytics with a Minor in Accounting — both from Grand Canyon University, Colangelo College of Business.

At Honeywell Aerospace, I built Tableau dashboards and automated reports that enhanced decision-making for engineering operations, and partnered with the EDM Automation team to standardize dashboards organization-wide. At Leslie's Pools, I designed a Power BI scorecard dashboard used across 950+ store locations, built SQL pipelines for operational data, and created Excel dashboards with advanced functions including VLOOKUP, XLOOKUP, and PivotTables.

My capstone project applied Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and Gradient Boosting in R alongside Monte Carlo simulations in Power BI to forecast SLA breaches and inventory risk — the kind of end-to-end thinking I bring to every dataset.

I'm currently studying for the FINRA Securities Industry Essentials (SIE) exam, and much of my recent independent work — client risk-tolerance scoring, portfolio correlation analysis, and Monte Carlo retirement planning — is focused on investment risk and financial decision-making.

Also completed self-directed coursework in Snowflake/dbt, MySQL, Tableau, and Power BI to round out my technical toolkit.
